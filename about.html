About ReelRank & how we test

ReelRank reviews AI video tools — generators, editors, and short-drama workflows. Our only job is to answer one question honestly: is this tool worth your money?

Our testing methodology

Every tool we review is scored on the same five criteria:

  1. Output quality — realism, artifacts, physics, and consistency across shots.
  2. Prompt adherence — does the model actually follow what you asked?
  3. Audio — quality and synchronization, when the model supports it.
  4. Speed & cost — generation time, credit consumption, and real per-clip economics.
  5. Workflow fit — editing tools, exports, platform support, and team features.

We run identical prompt sets across tools wherever possible, so scores are comparable. When a tool ships a major update, we re-run the test suite and update the review with a visible date stamp.

Transparency about review status

Every review page carries a status badge. Tested means our own hands-on test run is complete. Draft means the review is based on published benchmarks, arena data, and documentation, with hands-on testing in progress. Draft scores are provisional and can change after testing. We'd rather mark our homework incomplete than fake the results.

Editorial independence

  • Vendors cannot pay to change a rating or a ranking.
  • Any sponsored placement will be clearly labeled as such, and never affects our verdicts.
  • We use affiliate links where available — if you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That's what keeps this site free.
  • We only rank tools we can actually access and test. Tools we haven't tested are listed as "review coming" rather than ranked.
