Hailuo 3.0 Review

Long takes, narrative scenes and strong Chinese-language support from MiniMax.

★★★★☆ 4.0 / 5 Tested — Aug 2, 2026 Updated Aug 2, 2026

TL;DR — Our verdict

Hailuo 3.0 is MiniMax's strongest video model yet, earning its place in the top tier on long-take consistency and narrative scenes. It's particularly strong for Chinese-language content and delivers reliable output across resolutions. It doesn't match Veo 3.1's photorealism ceiling, but for storytellers who need sustained, coherent scenes it's a serious contender — and typically cheaper per clip than the cinematic leaders.

What is Hailuo 3.0?

Hailuo 3.0 is MiniMax's flagship video generation model, available through hailuoai.video. The Hailuo line is known for long takes and narrative scene structure, with particularly strong performance on Chinese-language prompts. It's positioned between the volume workhorse (Kling) and the cinematic leaders (Veo), offering consistency and storytelling chops at a competitive price.

What makes it the long-take pick

  • Long takes and narrative scenes — built for storytelling, not just single shots.
  • Strong Chinese-language understanding and output.
  • Good consistency across shots and settings.
  • Competitive credit pricing for sustained scenes.

Who should use it

Hailuo 3.0 suits narrative creators — storytellers, drama producers, and Chinese-language content teams who need sustained scenes rather than isolated clips. If your prompt work leans on long takes and emotional continuity, it's a strong value in the top tier.

What to watch out for

  • Photorealism ceiling is below Veo 3.1 on complex physics and light.
  • Credit-based pricing; long scenes consume more credits.
  • Smaller ecosystem than ByteDance's Dreamina/CapCut stack.

Alternatives to consider

  • Veo 3.1 — better photorealism and native audio for cinematic shots.
  • Seedance 2.5 — better long-form production workflows and ecosystem.
  • Kling 3.0 — cheaper for high-volume generation.

FAQ

How much does Hailuo 3.0 cost?

Hailuo is credit-based. Prices depend on resolution and duration; volume packs improve per-clip cost. Verify current rates on the official site.

Does Hailuo 3.0 have a free tier?

The platform offers free daily credits, enough to evaluate quality before paying.

How long can Hailuo 3.0 clips be?

Standard clips run about 10 seconds at 1080p; the model's strength is sustained, narrative scenes assembled from multiple takes.

Can I use Hailuo output commercially?

Paid use generally includes commercial rights under MiniMax's terms; check platform monetization rules before scaling.
