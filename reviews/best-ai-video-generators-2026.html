How we ranked them

Every tool is scored on the same five criteria: output quality (realism, artifacts, physics), prompt adherence, audio (quality and sync), speed & cost, and workflow fit (editing, exports, platform support). Scores shown are draft ratings based on published benchmarks, arena data, and documentation; each will be updated after our own hands-on test run, and re-tested whenever a major model ships.

Notes on specific picks

  • Veo 3.1 leads on human-preference benchmarks (MovieGenBench) for visual quality, prompt adherence, and physics realism — and it's the only top model with true native audio plus 2+ minute clips.
  • Kling 3.0 has the best per-clip economics and a long-form mode (up to ~5 minutes) with multilingual lip-sync — the volume player's pick.
  • Runway Gen-4 is the art director's tool: style references, camera control, and an editing suite. It costs more per second of output.
  • CapCut Director Mode targets the booming AI short-drama market with a full script-to-export workflow powered by Seedance 2.0.
  • Sora 2 caution: OpenAI has announced the Sora 2 API will sunset in September 2026 — don't build a pipeline on it without a migration plan.

Which one should you buy?

  • You make brand films or cinematic content: Veo 3.1
  • You need lots of clips on a budget: Kling 3.0
  • You art-direct and need control: Runway Gen-4
  • You publish reels, shorts, or AI dramas: CapCut Director Mode
  • You need long, production-style shots: Seedance 2.5
